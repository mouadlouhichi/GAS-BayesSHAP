# GAS-BayesSHAP Paper Results Summary (tight-ε, real data)

Generated by `scripts/run_paper_experiments.py` on real UCI data
(`winequality-white.csv`, `Beijing_MultiSite_AirQuality.csv`), with
**ε = 0.05** (not the vacuous 15.0 used in the smoke-test notebooks).

## RQ1 — Attribution fidelity (GAS vs exact Shapley, per-instance)

| Dataset | Clusters | N | GAS RMSE (mean±std) | KernelSHAP RMSE | SamplingSHAP RMSE | GAS evals | Exact evals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wine | 2 | 6 | **0.00170 ± 0.00076** | 0.00395 | 0.0531 | ~1029 | 2048 |
| Air (Aotizhongxin) | 4 | 5 | **0.00142 ± 0.00075** | 0.00366 | 0.0301 | ~1442 | 2048 |

→ GAS-BayesSHAP recovers exact Shapley at **RMSE ~1e-3** using **~50–70% of the
exact enumeration budget**, beating KernelSHAP (~2.5×) and SamplingSHAP
(~20–35×) at similar budgets.

## RQ2 — Anytime coverage (simultaneous)

| Dataset | Simultaneous coverage rate | Marginal coverage rate | Sign-certified fraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wine | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.0 |
| Air | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.0 |

- **Coverage holds** (1.0 across all instances) — the (1−δ) guarantee is
  empirically satisfied.
- **No feature is sign-certified** at ε=0.05 with budget ≤3000: the anytime
  widths are still large (mean ~9–17) because the empirical-Bernstein range
  term (`R_Δ = 4`) dominates. This is the honest cost of distribution-free
  anytime certification.

## Width-vs-budget tradeoff (wine; the paper's key honest curve)

| Budget (coalition evals) | Mean certified width | Sign-certified | Converged |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500 | ∞ (insufficient coverage) | 0.0 | no |
| 1000 | 24.7 | 0.0 | no |
| 2000 | 13.1 | 0.0 | no |
| 4000 | 7.1 | 0.0 | no |
| 8000 | 4.0 | 0.0 | no |

→ Width falls roughly as `1/√budget`; **sign-certification needs budgets
≥ 10^4–10^5** coalition evals for these games. The paper should present this
curve rather than claim tight intervals at 1k evals.

## What can / cannot be claimed

**Can claim (RQ1, RQ2):**
- Exact Shapley is computable at M=11 and GAS-BayesSHAP recovers it to RMSE
  ~1e-3 at ~half the exact budget.
- Simultaneous coverage ≥ 1−δ is empirically validated (1.0).
- Point estimates beat KernelSHAP and SamplingSHAP at matched budgets.

**Cannot yet claim (RQ3/RQ5):**
- Sign-certified domain drivers (needs budget ≥ 10^4–10^5).
- Query-efficiency superiority to KernelSHAP as a *certified* statement
  (needs matched-budget curves — see `run_paper_experiments.py --only curves`).
- The 4 air regimes need an explicit regime-labeling step (the run assigns
  k=4 clusters but names them only by index).
